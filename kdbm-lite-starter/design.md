# Design Direction

## Design Inspiration URL

Fallback (designmd.ai unavailable): Playful student dashboard

## What We Borrow

- **Layout:** Colorful card-based layout with clear sections
- **Spacing:** Generous spacing between cards and sections for breathing room
- **Color mood:** Soft, Grogu-inspired colors — warm browns, gentle greens, soft creams, light space purples and blues
- **Typography feel:** Friendly, rounded, approachable
- **Card/button style:** Playful buttons with rounded corners, soft shadows, and cute hover effects
- **Mobile feel:** Stacked cards, readable on phone width, touch-friendly

## What We Do Not Copy

- **Brand/logo:** No Grogu/Disney/Lucasfilm logos or branding — original Grogu-inspired art only
- **Content/copy:** All content original, not copied from any official source
- **Testimonials/stats:** No fake testimonials or fake stats
- **Photos/private identity:** No real photos of Grogu or Disney assets — use original illustrations/icons
- **Exact layout if it would look like a clone:** Adapt inspiration into unique Grogu-themed direction

## Visual Mood

Fun, friendly, adorable, cozy, space-themed

## Layout Rules

**Hero/first-screen:** Large Grogu illustration or character, site title, soft space background with floating stars, welcoming entry point

**Section rhythm:** Each section has breathing room, cards arranged in grids (desktop) or stacks (mobile), smooth transitions between sections via sticky nav

**Card/content block style:** Rounded cards with soft shadows, pastel/brown color tints, cute icons, consistent padding

**Primary CTA style:** Large, rounded, playful buttons (e.g., "Feed Grogu", "Play", "Pet Grogu") with clear labels and hover states

**Mobile first-screen:** Hero stacks vertically, nav collapses to hamburger menu, cards stack in single column, all tap targets readable

## Color / Contrast Rules

- Primary: Warm brown, soft cream background
- Accents: Gentle greens, soft purples, light space blues
- Must have readable contrast (WCAG AA minimum)
- Avoid overly saturated or neon colors — keep it comfortable to look at
- Space theme: dark navy or deep purple background for hero, lighter tones for content sections

## Typography Feel

Friendly, rounded, playful — approachable and easy to read. No overly formal or stiff fonts.

## Component Style

**Buttons:** Rounded, soft-shadowed, playful — primary actions in warm/bright accents, secondary in softer tones

**Cards:** Rounded corners, soft shadows, pastel/brown background tints, subtle hover lift effect

**Inputs:** Rounded, clearly labeled, spacious for touch targets

**Status chips / labels:** Small rounded badges with soft background colors

## Mobile Rules

- Hamburger menu for navigation
- Single-column card stacking
- All buttons and tap targets minimum 44px touch target
- Hero text and Grogu illustration readable at phone width
- Sections stack vertically, no horizontal scroll

## Accessibility Basics

- Readable contrast on all text and background combinations (WCAG AA)
- Visible focus states on interactive elements
- All buttons/inputs have clear labels
- No tiny tap targets — minimum 44px
- Alt text on all images
- Semantic HTML structure

## Anti-Slop Rules

- No fake logos
- No fake testimonials
- No fake stats unless clearly marked sample
- No lorem ipsum in final proof
- No random gradient blobs unless they match the space/Grogu direction
- One clear primary action per section
- Readable on phone width

- [ ] Nothing clones another brand
